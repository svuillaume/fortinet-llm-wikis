## Summary (first paragraph of source)

**Summary:** As of Jan 2026, FortiCloud-managed IAM users are no longer auto-synced into FortiCNAPP users. Instead, Permission Profiles sync from FortiCloud, and admins must manually assign Access Profiles in the FortiCNAPP UI to enable user access.
