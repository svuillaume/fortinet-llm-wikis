## Verbatim text

> FortiCloud Organizations
> FortiCNAPP can be configured into a FortiCloud Organization hierarchy.
>
> FortiCloud Organizations is a centralized account management service that consolidates multiple FortiCloud accounts into an Organization with Organizational Units (OUs). Accounts, assets, and users can be assigned to specific OUs with set permissions and scope to improve Organization hierarchy and access.
>
> The Organization portal can be selected from the Support dropdown menu. OUs can be accessed by selected when logging into the account and from the dropdown menu in the banner.
