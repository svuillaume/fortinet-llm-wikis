## Key facts

- Defines SSPM as continuous monitoring of SaaS configurations against benchmarks (misconfiguration, excess permissions, drift, compliance, shadow IT); covers SaaS only.
- Five functions: continuous monitoring, security gap analysis, compliance posture assessment (NIST CSF, GDPR, CCPA, PCI DSS), alerts and remediation, dashboards.
- Core capabilities: misconfiguration management, identity and access governance, third-party app management, compliance monitoring, threat detection.
- Use cases: SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / HIPAA readiness, M&A SaaS assessment, insider threat, third-party OAuth app risk.
- Positioning vs adjacent categories: SSPM inward-facing and preventive; CSPM = cloud infrastructure; CASB = access and data in transit; SIEM = log correlation, reactive.
- Cited stats (CSA State of SaaS Security 2025-2026): 21% "very confident" they can detect all SaaS apps in use; 46% struggle with non-human identities / SaaS-to-SaaS integrations; 44% struggle with distributed SaaS administration. IBM: $4.44M average breach cost (2025). Wiz research: 54% of cloud environments have exposed VMs with access to sensitive data.
- **Wiz states it is not an SSPM tool.** It is a cloud/AI security platform that "integrates natively with leading SSPM solutions" and surfaces their findings in its risk graph next to cloud, identity and data risk. Demo CTA names Salesforce, Google Workspace and Snowflake as SaaS examples.
- Customer example: Siemens, cloud visibility 20% to 100% (Wiz claim).
