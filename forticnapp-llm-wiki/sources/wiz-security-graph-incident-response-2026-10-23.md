## Summary

**Wiz claims (vendor blog, not measured fact).** Extracted through a summarizing fetch on 2026-10-23, so wording is limited to the quoted phrases below; the full page text was not stored.

- Data model: the Security Graph is "a graph database that meticulously simulates your cloud infrastructure" and "captures real-time events from your cloud provider"; Wiz calls it a "digital twin of your environment."
- It "maps out the relationships and dependencies" among resources and adds runtime context from the Wiz sensor plus "the ingestion of cloud events."
- Use in incident response: root cause analysis and blast radius analysis are "based on the Wiz Security Graph"; it can "trace potential attack paths" and shows which other resources are at risk. Wiz CDR adds recent activity on the compromised resource; forensics collects volume copies and a "Forensics Package."
- Kubernetes: Wiz "has open-sourced our blast radius calculation method" (NamespaceHound).
- **Not mentioned:** graph neural networks, machine learning, anomaly detection or behavioral baselines. The page says Wiz is "continuously monitoring your workloads for suspicious activity in runtime" without explaining how suspicious is determined.
