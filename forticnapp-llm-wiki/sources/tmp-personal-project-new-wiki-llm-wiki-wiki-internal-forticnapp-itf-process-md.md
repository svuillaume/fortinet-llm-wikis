## Summary (first paragraph of source)

**Summary:** The Integration Test Framework (ITF) process enables customers and partners to provision FortiCNAPP tenants through Fortinet's self-service portal. It involves requesting a license via ITF, registering it in FortiCloud, and provisioning the tenant with the master account.
