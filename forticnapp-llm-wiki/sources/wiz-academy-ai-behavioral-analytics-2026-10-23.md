## Summary

**Mixed page: generic education plus Wiz product claims.** Extracted through a summarizing fetch on 2026-10-23; full text not stored.

Wiz product claims:
- Within Wiz Defend, behavioral analytics builds baselines for users, service accounts, workloads and data resources.
- Wiz correlates runtime signals with control-plane events, configuration data and identity context, and prioritizes unusual behavior that intersects with excessive permissions, public exposure or sensitive data access.
- The SecOps AI Agent automatically investigates new threats; it is "trained on Wiz's internal incident response knowledge base," shows its reasoning, and analyst feedback refines later investigations.
- Wiz calls its approach "system-focused rather than model-focused" and says it avoids "opaque scoring."

Generic education (not Wiz-specific): behavioral analytics learns normal and flags deviations; needs context such as permissions, exposure and data sensitivity; typical challenges are concept drift, lack of context, explainability and scale (peer-group baselining and periodic retraining are standard mitigations).

**Not mentioned:** graph neural networks, the Security Graph, or any specific ML architecture or algorithm.
