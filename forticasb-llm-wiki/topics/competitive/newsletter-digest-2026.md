- 2025-10-30: ServiceNow patches most hosted instances for BodySnatcher (CVE-2025-12420, CVSS 9.3). [src: casb-sspm-ci-newsletter-2026-08-17]
- November 2025: Gainsight connected-app token abuse; Salesforce revokes Gainsight tokens. AppOmni ships AgentGuard for ServiceNow AI agents. [src: competitive-analysis-forticasb-sspm-2026-08] [src: casb-sspm-ci-newsletter-2026-08-17]
- Q4 2025: Wiz reportedly adds SSPM through a stealth acquisition (Verified in source). [src: casb-sspm-newsletter-sep2026]
